# LAN Worlds

A mod for BTA that allows you to easily turn your worlds into LAN servers!

## Dependencies

- [Halplibe 5.3.1](https://github.com/Turnip-Labs/bta-halplibe)
- [Raywire 1.0.5](https://github.com/Apollointhehouse/raywire)
- [Fabric Language Kotlin 1.13.7](https://github.com/FabricMC/fabric-language-kotlin)

## Usage

- Place the mod and its dependencies into the mods folder
- Open a world
- Go to settings, general, and scroll down until you find "Open to LAN"
- Click on "Open to Lan"
